## Trichoplax adhaerens

Placozoans are tiny marine animals with an exceptionally simple body organization. The best-known species, *Trichoplax adhaerens*, is only a few millimetres across and lacks conventional organs, muscles, and nervous tissue. Despite this simplicity, placozoans possess a surprisingly complex repertoire of genes involved in cell differentiation and developmental processes.

## Research

This project investigates Placozoa as a model system for understanding fundamental questions in animal evolution, cell biology, and developmental biology.
