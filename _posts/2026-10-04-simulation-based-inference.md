## Simulation-Based Inference (SBI)

Simulation-based inference provides a framework for performing statistical inference when the likelihood of a generative model is difficult or impossible to evaluate explicitly.

## Research

This project explores simulation-based methods for connecting computational models with observed data, with a focus on parameter inference, model comparison, and uncertainty quantification.
